A GUIDE TO YOUR ENQUIRY

Before
we begin.

Useful things to think about, whether you have a clear plan or are just exploring the options.

Talk it through ↗
01

Describe the concern, not just the cut

Is a tree taking up more space than you expected? Are you worried about a particular branch, or considering removal as part of a garden change? Explain what has prompted your enquiry.

A photograph of the whole tree and another showing the area around its base can give useful context. Only take photographs from somewhere you can safely stand; there is no need to climb or approach a tree you are concerned about.

02

Hedge care, pruning and removal

Keeping a hedge tidy, changing its size and removing it altogether are different jobs. County provides hedge cutting and pruning, and can discuss hedge removal when you want a different use for the space.

Tell us the approximate height and length, whether one or both sides need attention, and what access is available. If the hedge is being removed, explain what you intend to put in its place and discuss the handling of roots and cut material.

03

Stumps, roots and the complete job

Stump grinding and root removal are also available. These can be discussed following tree removal or as work on a stump that is already in the garden.

Mention nearby walls, paving, planting and any known underground services. Agree the work on the stump or roots, what material will be removed and how the area will be left. These services should be included explicitly in the quote, rather than assumed to come with felling.

TREES & HEDGES / QUESTIONS

A few
useful answers.

Can you remove a stump that is already there?

Yes. Stump grinding and root removal are offered. Tell us its approximate size and position, how it can be accessed and what you want to do with the area afterwards.

Do you remove hedges as well as trim them?

Yes. Explain whether you want a trim, pruning or full hedge removal. Agree the treatment of roots and removal of material as part of the scope.

Does tree removal automatically include the stump?

No — make sure it is included in the agreed scope if you need it. County offers stump grinding and root removal, so you can discuss these alongside the tree work.
