Planning your gardens & landscaping?

A place for the table. A clear route to the shed. A lawn the children can use. The best starting point for a garden change is how you want to spend time in it.

Area, ground preparation, access, materials and the amount of existing garden to remove all shape the scope. A photograph starts the conversation; it may not show everything needed for a final quote.

Discuss your job ↗

Helpful details for your quote

  • A few photographs from opposite ends of the garden
  • Approximate width and length of the area
  • What you want to keep, change or remove
  • Access, levels and any wet areas
  • Your priorities, budget range and preferred timing

Don’t have every detail? Give us a call and we’ll talk it through.

Your questions answered

Ask us directly ↗
Can you look after my garden regularly?

Yes. County offers regular maintenance programmes. Tell us the size of the garden and the work you need help keeping on top of, then discuss the visit frequency and tasks to include.

Do you supply the turf as well as lay it?

Yes — turf supply and installation are both offered. Mention the approximate area, the condition of the existing ground and how the garden is accessed.

Can a clearance be followed by a patio or new lawn?

Yes. Bring up the full plan at the start so the clearance, preparation and new work can be discussed together, including anything you want to retain.
