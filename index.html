BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A few useful answers.

Can I get a free quote?

Yes. Call 07526 024115 and tell us about the work you have in mind. Include the service, your postcode and an idea of the size of the job.

Do you cover my area?

Call us with your town or postcode to check whether we can help at your address.

Can I ask about more than one service?

Of course. If your garden needs a combination of landscaping, fencing, tree care, hedge trimming or pressure washing, tell us about the whole job.

What information should I have ready?

Your postcode, a description of the work, approximate measurements if you have them, and your preferred timing are useful starting points. Mention any access restrictions too.
