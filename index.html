BEFORE WE PICK UP THE TOOLS

A little thought now.
A clearer plan later.

A useful enquiry doesn’t need to be long. These are the details that help turn an idea into a practical conversation.

01 — THE SPACE

Give us the bigger picture.

Your postcode, a description of the area and rough measurements help. Mention slopes, narrow gates or anything that makes access unusual.

02 — THE PRIORITY

Tell us what matters most.

Separate the must-haves from the nice-to-haves. If there’s a comfortable budget range, share it so the conversation starts in the right place.

03 — THE SCOPE

Talk through the complete job.

Ask what’s included, what needs preparing, what happens to removed material and how the space will be left. Agree the details before booking.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
get in touch.

Do you cover my area?

Call 07526 024115 with your town or postcode to check whether County can help at your address. Include the type of job you have in mind.

Is the quote free?

Yes — call for a free quote. Explain the work and the space so the team can discuss whether further details or a visit are needed.

Can I discuss several jobs at once?

Yes. Mention all the work you are considering, even if you want to tackle it in stages. Fencing, landscaping and garden care can affect one another, so the broader picture is useful.

How much detail do I need before I call?

A description, your postcode and an idea of the size of the job are enough to begin. You can discuss which measurements or photographs would be helpful after that.

What affects the price?

The amount of work, access, materials, existing condition and what needs removing all play a part. Each service guide explains the details worth discussing for that type of job.

Can I choose a particular date?

Let County know your preferred timing and any deadlines. Availability and the agreed scope need to be confirmed directly before you make arrangements.
