Planning your fencing & boundaries?

A fence sets the edge of your garden and the feel of the space inside it. Whether you are replacing a tired run or planning a new boundary, begin with what you need it to do.

Length, chosen materials, post requirements, ground conditions and removal of existing fencing affect the work. A clear description makes it easier to compare the options being discussed.

Discuss your job ↗

Helpful details for your quote

  • Approximate length or number of panels
  • Photos showing the full run and existing posts
  • Your preferred appearance and privacy needs
  • Access to each side of the fence
  • Whether old fencing needs to be removed

Don’t have every detail? Give us a call and we’ll talk it through.

Your questions answered

Ask us directly ↗
Do you do brick walls as well as fencing?

Yes. Brickwork and garden walls are part of County’s boundary services. Explain the size, purpose and position of the proposed work so the requirements can be discussed.

Can you paint or treat an existing fence?

Yes. Fence painting and treatment are offered. Tell us the approximate length, which sides need doing, the existing finish and any areas that need repair first.

Can I ask about a small repair?

Yes. Describe the damaged section and the condition of the adjacent fence. Photos of the posts and the full run will help the initial conversation.
