A GUIDE TO YOUR ENQUIRY

Before
we begin.

Useful things to think about, whether you have a clear plan or are just exploring the options.

Talk it through ↗
01

Think beyond the panel

Privacy, appearance and the relationship with the rest of the garden are all worth discussing. Bring a reference picture if you have a particular style in mind, and explain what is not working about the current fence.

A boundary beside a seating area might have different priorities from one alongside a side passage. Mention any changes in ground level or awkward corners along the run.

02

Fencing, brickwork or a garden wall

A boundary can be more than a fence. County also provides brickwork and garden walls, so explain the look and purpose you have in mind before settling on a finish.

Include approximate length and height, changes in level, the existing boundary and access. Say if a wall would need to hold back soil rather than simply mark the edge of a space, so the requirements can be discussed.

03

Repairs and care for what is already there

Not every boundary needs replacing. Fencing repairs, painting and treatment can also be discussed, depending on the condition of the existing timber and structure.

Photographs of the whole run and any damaged areas help. Mention previous finishes, planting close to the fence and access to each side. Agree preparation, materials and which faces are included before booking.

FENCING & BOUNDARIES / QUESTIONS

A few
useful answers.

Do you do brick walls as well as fencing?

Yes. Brickwork and garden walls are part of County’s boundary services. Explain the size, purpose and position of the proposed work so the requirements can be discussed.

Can you paint or treat an existing fence?

Yes. Fence painting and treatment are offered. Tell us the approximate length, which sides need doing, the existing finish and any areas that need repair first.

Can I ask about a small repair?

Yes. Describe the damaged section and the condition of the adjacent fence. Photos of the posts and the full run will help the initial conversation.
