Privacy information

This website provides information about County Landscapes and ways to ask about its services.

Contacting County Landscapes

For enquiries, email countylandscaping77@gmail.com, call 07526 024115, or use the online enquiry form.

Online enquiries

When you submit the form, your name, contact details, postcode, chosen service, preferred timing and message are sent through Formspree to County Landscapes so the business can respond to your enquiry. Please only include information needed to discuss the requested work.

Website services

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider receives technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests website resources. Formspree processes online enquiry submissions. Fonts are supplied by your device. This website does not add analytics or advertising cookies.

External links

The TikTok link opens an external service, which has its own privacy practices.

Questions

If you have a question about information you have shared with County Landscapes, call 07526 024115.
