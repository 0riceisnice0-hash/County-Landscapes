Privacy information

This website provides information about County Landscapes and ways to ask about its services.

Contacting County Landscapes

For enquiries, email countylandscaping77@gmail.com or call 07526 024115. The online enquiry form is currently a preview and does not send or store the details you enter.

When online enquiries are connected

The form is prepared to use Formspree. Once enabled, the name, contact details, postcode, chosen service and message you submit will be sent through Formspree to County Landscapes to respond to your enquiry. This page should be reviewed and updated by the business before enabling the form.

Website services

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider receives technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests website resources. Fonts are supplied by your device. This website does not add analytics, advertising cookies or browser storage.

External links

The TikTok link opens an external service, which has its own privacy practices.

Questions

If you have a question about information you have shared with County Landscapes, call 07526 024115.
